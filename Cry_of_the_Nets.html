Cry of the Nets

By Allen Ginsberg

May 1956

San Francisco Literary Archive, Beat Poetry Vault

I saw Wharf sailors roar at dawn’s edge, their fists full of kelp and starlight’s howl, fishwives chanting psalms of salt and storm, torches blazing in alleys born of error! Oh Fisherman Heights, you pulse with tide, a city’s wild veins screaming your name, gulls shrieking to the moon’s raw face, a borough of untamed hope and spray!
Note: Found in an online archive of Ginsberg’s early drafts, this poem reflects the raw energy of San Francisco’s Beat movement. Digitized from a handwritten notebook in the SF Literary Archive’s Beat collection.

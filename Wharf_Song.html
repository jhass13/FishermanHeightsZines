Wharf Song

By Lawrence Ferlinghetti

July 1954

San Francisco Literary Archive, Bay Area Poets Series

The Wharf’s nets gleam with dusk’s wet light, foghorns chanting secrets to the bay. Poets scribble on driftwood tables, their words dancing in Fisherman Heights’ crab-shell dreams. A map, sketched wrong, birthed this slope of neon and tides, its pulse alive in the gull’s sharp cry, carrying the bay’s wild heart ashore.
Note: Discovered in a digitized collection of Ferlinghetti’s unpublished manuscripts, this poem captures the vibrant maritime culture of San Francisco’s waterfront. Scanned from a typescript held at the SF Literary Archive.

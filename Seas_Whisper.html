Sea’s Whisper

By Kenneth Rexroth

April 1948

San Francisco Literary Archive, Early Bay Poets

Moonlight strokes the Wharf’s dark piers, waves weaving tales of forgotten nets. Fishers hum hymns older than stone, their voices blending with the bay’s sigh. No marker tells of a chart’s mistake, yet Fisherman Heights holds shanties tight, its name a low song the sea still hums, drifting to the stars’ eternal hush.
Note: Uncovered in a digitized archive of Rexroth’s poetry, this piece evokes the serene beauty of San Francisco’s coastal landscapes. Transcribed from a typed draft in the SF Literary Archive’s early collections.
